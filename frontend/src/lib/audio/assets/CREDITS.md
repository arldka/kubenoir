## Notes

- **Tickeys** is declared MIT licensed by its authors, but its repository has no `LICENSE` file, so
  there is no upstream license text to copy here. The recordings are credited to the project and
  linked above.
- **Ambience** is optional and not shipped with the repository. If you add an `ambience.*` file, it
  must be CC0, CC-BY or your own, and it must be listed in the table above. Local copies are
  gitignored.
